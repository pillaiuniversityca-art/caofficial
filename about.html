The Computer Association at Pillai University represents a vibrant, self-governing ecosystem where theoretical computer science meets cutting-edge innovation, peer mentorship, and visionary leadership.

OUR QUARTER-CENTURY HERITAGE

TRANSFORMING CURIOSITY
INTO ROYAL EXCELLENCE

Since its founding in 2002, the Computer Association has remained the cornerstone of technical culture across the School of Computing at Pillai University.

What started as an enthusiastic circle of coding enthusiasts has grown into an autonomous, 100% student-governed powerhouse delivering annual state-level technical festivals, industry-certified workshops, high-octane hackathons, and career-defining mentorship networks.

As we celebrate our historic 25 Years Silver Jubilee (2002–2027), we look forward to the royal horizon of BITFEST 2027—where arcade-born problem-solving meets modern artificial intelligence and quantum paradigms.
