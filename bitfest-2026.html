What is BITFEST & How Bitfest 2026 Successfully Concluded

Pillai University's Premier Annual Tech Fest

BITFEST is the flagship annual technical festival organized by the Computer Association, School of Computing at Pillai University. It brings together engineering innovators, competitive coders, designers, and tech enthusiasts to solve real-world problems.

Bitfest 2026 has concluded with resounding success! Over two electrifying days (09th & 10th September 2026), the festival welcomed 500+ participants, hosted an intense 8-Hour Mini Hackathon, debugging sprints, AI prompt engineering challenges, and 4 certified industry masterclasses in Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, and Generative AI.
