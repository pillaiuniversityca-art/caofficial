25 Years of Innovation: The Silver Jubilee Legacy of Computer Association

"25 Years. Countless Ideas. One Unbroken Community."
Krishna K. Singh
HEAD, COMPUTER ASSOCIATION
4 MIN READ • 25 YEARS SILVER JUBILEE

In 2002, the technological horizon was drastically different. Dial-up modems still hummed, early web portals were in their infancy, and mobile computing was barely a whisper. Yet on the Pillai University campus, forward-looking computing pioneers recognized that software and algorithms would reshape civilization.

From that initial seed grew the Computer Association—an ecosystem dedicated to student autonomy, technical inquiry, and real-world mastery that has thrived for an unbroken quarter of a century (25 Years).

1. The Synergy of Our 7 Departments

What makes CA unique is our autonomous operational structure: Publicity, Social Media, Graphics, Content, Creatives, MIS, and Coverage. Each department operates with professional rigor, providing students with executive leadership experience that mirrors top technology organizations.

2. Unbroken Alumni Legacy

Over 10,000 alumni trace their roots back to Computer Association hackathons, technical conferences, and student committees. Today, they lead engineering teams at global tech giants, launch disruptive startups, and mentor incoming freshers.

3. Step Into BITFEST 2027

As Bitfest 2026 participants download their hard-earned certificates, we are already orchestrating our grandest celebration yet: BITFEST 2027. Prepare to be wowed by our Silver Jubilee spectacle.
